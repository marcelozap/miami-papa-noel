How It Works

No payment until we confirm the visit.

Send the request first. We confirm the time, final price, and travel. Then a deposit saves the date.

Deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit locks your date. The balance is due on arrival.

Zelle to 305-244-0360. Put the event date and your name in the memo.

One free reschedule if a named storm or Papa Noel himself forces it.

A date is not reserved until the deposit clears.
