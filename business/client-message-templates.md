# Client Message Templates

The booking lifecycle, first reply to referral ask. Every template is a matched EN/ES pair —
**reply in the language the enquiry arrived in.**

Rates come from the locked card in `lead-reply-bank.md` and `AGENT-ROLES.md`. Never improvise a number.
Deposit is **50% non-refundable, Zelle to 305-244-0360, memo = event date + client name.** No other
payment method is offered.

---

## New Inquiry Reply

English:

```text
Hi [NAME], thank you for reaching out about Papa Noel.

Send me these and I can confirm availability and the right option:

- Event date and start time
- City or address area
- Event type (home visit, community event, school, corporate, photo session)
- Approximate number of children and adults
- How long you need Santa
- Whether Santa will hand out gifts

Papa Noel works in Spanish and English, so tell me which the families will prefer.
```

Spanish:

```text
Hola [NAME], gracias por escribir sobre Papa Noel.

Enviame estos datos y le confirmo disponibilidad y la mejor opcion:

- Fecha del evento y hora de inicio
- Ciudad o zona de la direccion
- Tipo de evento (visita a casa, evento comunitario, escuela, corporativo, sesion de fotos)
- Cantidad aproximada de ninos y adultos
- Cuanto tiempo necesitan a Papa Noel
- Si Papa Noel va a entregar regalos

Papa Noel trabaja en espanol e ingles, asi que digame cual prefieren las familias.
```
