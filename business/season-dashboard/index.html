3Booking rules

These come from the published checkout page and the project's booking process.

Deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit locks the date. The balance is due on arrival. Paid by Zelle to 305-244-0360, with the event date and the client's name in the memo.

Un deposito no reembolsable del 50% asegura la fecha. El saldo se paga al llegar.

The line that decides everything

A date is not reserved until the deposit clears. Booking is confirmed only after availability, deposit, address, parking details and calendar confirmation. An inquiry, a quote, a "yes we want it", even a screenshot of a payment — none of those are a booking.

Reschedule

One free reschedule if a named storm or Papa Noel himself forces it.

Dates that fill first

2026-12-12 · 2026-12-13 · 2026-12-19 · 2026-12-20 · 2026-12-24 — held first-come with a deposit, never discounted.

6Save & restore

The browser is not a backup. Your ticks, statuses and notes are stored inside this one browser on this one device. Clearing history, using a different browser or phone, or opening the file in private mode will show none of it. Export a CSV at the end of every working day and keep it somewhere real.

Export writes a CSV file to your Downloads. Import reads one back in and replaces what is in the browser now.
