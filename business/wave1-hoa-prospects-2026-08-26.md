## The finding that changes the pitch

**"Lifestyle" is the qualifying filter, not size** — and it splits this market cleanly in two.

**About a third of these firms publish an actual lifestyle services division or lifestyle director role**
(Castle, Marquis, Premier, FirstService, Vesta, Campbell). Those firms already have a budget line, a
calendar, and a named human whose job is booking December entertainment. The ask is simply:

> *"Who is your lifestyle director, and are they booked for December yet?"*

**The other two-thirds publish only financial, compliance and maintenance language** (Allied, FPMS, IPMS,
Apex, Landmark, Phoenix, New Horizons, Oasis). There the *association board* decides on a Santa, not the
manager — so the pitch is different: **offer to be a listed preferred vendor the manager can hand to
boards.** It costs the manager nothing, which makes it a far easier yes.

**Phone is the primary channel.** Only about half these firms publish a general email. The
**CAI Southeast Florida business directory** was by far the best source of *named humans with direct
emails* (Marquis, Campbell, Trident, Veridian, RealManage, Associa all had one) — working that directory
further is the highest-yield next research step.

**Drop these from the campaign:** Sentry Management has no South Florida office (14 Florida offices, none
in Miami-Dade or Broward). Lang Management stops at Boca Raton. Empire, Beacon and Sunstate are
Orlando/Sarasota operators that appear in Florida-wide search results.

**Verify before mailing:** the CAI listings for Trident and Associa both show the identical address
"304 Indian Trace, Suite 538, Weston" — almost certainly a directory artifact. Trust the phone and email
from those listings, verify the address.

**Lead with bilingual, don't mention it.** None of these firms advertise bilingual *programming*, but
several advertise bilingual *service* — which tells you the resident base already expects it. Doral and
West Miami-Dade are dense with mid-size, Spanish-first managed communities, and four of them (Affinity,
KWPMC, Unlimited PM, Miami Management) are within a few miles of Doral.
