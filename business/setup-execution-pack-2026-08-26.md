## 1 — Google Business Profile

~45 minutes of work, then up to 5 business days of Google's review.

1. Go to **https://business.google.com/add** → "Add your business to Google." Free.

2. **Business name: type `Miami Papa Noel` and nothing else.** Not "Miami Papa Noel — Bilingual Santa
   Claus Doral." Google: *"Including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted, and
   could result in the suspension of your Business Profile"* — taglines, service details, location,
   phone, URLs, ALL CAPS. **This is the number one cause of rejection for entertainer listings.**
   Keywords go in the description and services, never the name.

3. **Primary category: `Entertainer`.** A real, verbatim GBP category. There is no "Santa Claus"
   category.

4. **Secondary categories: `Children's party service` and `Entertainment agency`.** Do *not* add
   `Costume rental service` — you don't rent costumes, and a category-to-reality mismatch is a
   suspension trigger.

5. **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → NO.** You still enter the Doral address
   privately so Google can verify; it won't display publicly.

6. **Set the service area by naming places, not a radius** — Google requires this. Enter Miami-Dade
   County and Broward County; if the picker won't take counties, enter: Doral, Miami, Hialeah, Coral
   Gables, Miami Beach, Kendall, Homestead, Aventura, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines,
   Weston, Davie, Coral Springs, Sunrise, Miramar. You are safely inside Google's limit (the rule is
   only that the area shouldn't extend beyond ~2 hours' drive).

7. **Expect video verification.** Service-area businesses with a hidden residential address are
   routinely routed to video recording. Review takes up to 5 business days.

8. **Shoot it right the first time.** Recorded *inside* the verification flow on your phone — no
   uploads — as **one continuous take, no pauses or edits**, 60–120 seconds. Show three things:
   (a) a street sign or house number matching the address you gave; (b) that you control the location —
   unlock the front door with your key on camera; (c) that the business is real — the Santa suit,
   business cards, anything with "Miami Papa Noel" on it.

9. **Do a practice run on the normal camera app first.** Multiple failed attempts in 2026 can trigger a
   "No More Ways to Verify" message that requires contacting Google support before you can retry.

10. **Fill the ranking fields, in this order:** primary category · **Services** — add each one
    separately: Bilingual Santa Visit, Santa Home Visit, Corporate Santa, School Santa Visit, Santa
    Photo Session, Visita de Papá Noel · **Description** (750 chars) — lead with "bilingual
    Spanish/English Santa Claus serving Miami-Dade and Broward since 2017" · **Photos** — 10 minimum,
    in costume, real families, no stock · **Hours** — set seasonal hours rather than leaving blank ·
    **Phone** 305-244-0360 and **Website** miamipapanoel.com must match the site digit for digit ·
    **Reviews** — start asking the day you go live.

11. **Suspension triggers to avoid:** a PO box or unstaffed virtual office · a keyword-stuffed name ·
    a description with misspellings, gimmicky characters, gibberish, or heavy emphasis on prices and
    offers · personal contact details or ID numbers in the description.

## 2 — GigSalad and The Bash

~2 hours to build both properly. Most of it is writing and choosing photos.

### GigSalad — do this one first, the free tier is real

1. Sign up at **https://www.gigsalad.com/join**.

2. **The fee math:** service fee is **5% for free members, 2.5% for paid** — and *"only applied when a
   booking is confirmed through the site. You will not be charged for receiving leads or sending
   quotes."* Event planners separately pay 10–12% on top of your quote.

3. **Paid tiers:** Pro 3mo $139 / 6mo $229 / 12mo $359. Featured 3mo $169 / 6mo $289 / 12mo $479.
   All auto-renew, and coupons discount only the first payment.

4. **Recommendation: free now, Featured by 1 October.** GigSalad claims Featured members get roughly
   **20–28× more leads** than free, and only paid members can see a client's phone number before
   booking. Your whole season is Nov–Dec, so a 3-month Featured ($169) bought in October covers the
   money window — while starting free today accrues profile age and reviews at zero cost.

5. **Approval clock — this is why you sign up today.** Free memberships activate in **1–3 business
   days**; paid memberships go live **immediately**. If 31 Aug is a hard "live by" date, paying makes
   you live instantly.

6. **Signup gotchas:** the **Act/Service Name is PERMANENT once set** — type `Miami Papa Noel`
   carefully. Profile photo must be a real high-quality photo — *no logos, text, or AI images*, and an
   AI Santa image will get you rejected. Service overview is capped at **700 characters**.

7. **Category:** Variety → Santa Claus. Your competitive page is live at
   **https://www.gigsalad.com/Variety/Santa-Claus/FL/Miami**

8. **Price against the actual Miami market.** That page currently shows **34 Santas serving Miami**:
   The Rock Star Santa **$375 and up** (5.0, 42 reviews) · Santa South Florida / Johnny Mack **$275 &
   up** (4.95, 19 reviews) · Santa and Mrs. Claus for Hire **$350 and up** · Elkcim Entertainment
   **$300 and up**. **Set your starting price at $295–$325** — inside the pack, above the bottom.
   Platform-wide the average Santa rating is 4.9, so a 4.9 is table stakes, not an achievement.

9. **What converts:** 15–25 photos of real gigs with real families (the Miami leaders all lead with
   in-action shots, not studio portraits) · **one 30–60 second video of you in character speaking both
   Spanish and English** — this is the single biggest differentiator and almost no competitor has it ·
   **at least 5 reviews before 1 November**, requested from 2024–25 clients during signup · a visible
   starting price (listings without one get skipped) · the words bilingual, Spanish, español, Doral,
   Miami-Dade and Broward inside the 700 characters.

11. **Booking fee, from their own document:** *"A 5% ($20 minimum) booking fee is charged to vendors on

14. **Turn Auto-Add ON.** The Bash states **almost 50% of bookings come from auto-added leads.** Leaving

### Other marketplaces, in priority order

## 3 — Insurance and background check

### Insurance — buy today, ~30 minutes

1. **https://www.insurancecanopy.com/entertainment-insurance/performers/santa-claus** — a
   Santa-specific product page. **$18.50/month or $199/year** annual; **$59/event** for 1–3 days.

2. **Limits meet every school and venue requirement you will face:** General Liability **$2,000,000
   aggregate / $1,000,000 each occurrence**, $1M personal & advertising injury, $5,000 medical expense,
   $300,000 damage to premises rented.

3. **Buy annual, not per-event — the additional-insured math decides it.** Annual includes **unlimited
   additional insureds at no charge**; event policies charge **$5 each** ($10 for unlimited). Every
   school, church, HOA and mall that books you will demand to be named. One December pays for the year
   many times over.

4. **Santa is an explicitly covered class** — Insurance Canopy lists "Santas" and "Fairytale Characters"
   by name among eligible performer types.

5. **Certificates are same-day and self-serve:** *"Get insured in minutes and download your certificate
   instantly."* A school emails asking for a COI naming them; you log in, add them, download the PDF,
   reply. Minutes, no agent call.

6. **Optional add-ons (annual only):** equipment coverage +$7.50/mo for $2K or +$15/mo for $5K — worth
   it if the suit, props and camera gear exceed $2,000 to replace. Skip cyber liability.

**Fallbacks, only if Canopy declines you:**

7. **Sadler Sports** (sadlersports.com/entertainerinsurance, 800-622-7370) — published rate card:
   annual, income ≤$30K **$231 per performer**; single event ≤10 days **$181**. More expensive than
   Canopy for your income band.
8. **Francis L. Dean & Associates** (800-745-2409, info@fdean.com) — **lists "Santas" among covered
   performers** and, uniquely, offers **abuse coverage as an option**. Some Florida districts demand
   abuse/molestation coverage specifically, which basic GL excludes. **Call these people if a school's
   contract asks for it.** Annual premium not published.
9. **Skip Thimble** — no published prices, and event insurance is currently unavailable there.

### Background check — the obvious path is closed, read this before spending anything

10. **You cannot register for Florida's VECHS program as an individual.** FDLE's eligibility guide:
    the applicant *"Must be an organization, not an individual."* **The school or daycare is the
    qualified entity — they submit you.** Don't waste a day trying to enroll the business.

11. **Know the law, because it makes you sound professional to a principal.** Fla. Stat. **1012.465**
    (the Jessica Lunsford Act) requires Level 2 screening for noninstructional contractual personnel
    with direct student contact, and says the cost *"may be borne by the district school board, the
    contractor, or the person fingerprinted."* **Offer to pay it yourself — it closes bookings.**

12. **Exact state fees** (FDLE chart, effective 1 Jan 2025): **VECHS Employee $24 state + $36 federal =
    $60.** VECHS Volunteer $18 + $10 = **$28**. Name-based public check $24, federal not allowed.

13. **What to do this week:** get Live Scan fingerprinted at an FDLE-approved vendor so results are on
    file before anyone asks. **IDENTICO**, 4012 SW 18th St, West Park FL 33023 — ~25 min from Doral,
    **$60 employee / $50 volunteer**, appointment 10–15 minutes, results typically **24–72 hours**.
    Mon–Fri 9:00–4:15 (Fri closes 3:15), **(954) 239-8590**; they also do mobile fingerprinting.
    **Call first and say you're a contractor needing Level 2 for school work** — they'll tell you which
    ORI number to use, and the wrong ORI means paying twice.

14. **When a specific school books you, ask exactly this:** *"Do you want me to run Level 2 under your
    VECHS account, or does Miami-Dade County Public Schools require its own vendor badge?"* M-DCPS runs
    a separate vendor fingerprint-and-badge process. Their badge fee could not be verified (forms are
    behind a blocked robots.txt) — check **hb1877.dadeschools.net**. Budget for possibly paying twice.

15. **For private and corporate clients** who just want reassurance, use **ScreenMeNow** — the
    Santa-industry-standard vendor HireSanta partners with. Being screened through it is a credential
    you can name in marketing.

16. **Put it on the website** once you have both: *"Fully insured — $1M/$2M general liability. Level 2
    background screened. Certificate available on request."* This converts school and corporate
    inquiries more than any photo will.

## 4 — Search Console and the Vercel sitemap

~25 minutes, plus up to 24 hours of DNS propagation.

1. **First confirm the sitemap exists** — open `https://miamipapanoel.com/sitemap.xml`. If it 404s,
   stop and generate one before continuing.

2. Go to **https://search.google.com/search-console** → Add Property.

3. **Choose "Domain", not "URL prefix."** Enter `miamipapanoel.com` — no `https://`, no `www`. A Domain
   property covers all protocols and subdomains, which matters because Vercel serves both apex and www.

4. **Copy the whole TXT value** Google gives you (`google-site-verification=…`). Leave the tab open.

5. **Check how the domain is wired:** run `dig NS miamipapanoel.com +short`.

   **If it shows `ns1.vercel-dns.com` / `ns2.vercel-dns.com`** — add the record in Vercel:
   Dashboard → **Domains** → `miamipapanoel.com` → Advanced Settings → enable Vercel DNS if off → add
   record. **Name** = blank or `@` · **Type** = `TXT` · **Value** = the full string · **TTL** = leave
   the 60s default · **Comment** = "GSC verification" so you don't delete it by accident later.

   **If it shows GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.** — add the TXT at that registrar instead, host
   `@`. **Do not move nameservers to Vercel just for this** — you would have to re-add every existing
   DNS record, and a botched switch takes your email down mid-season.

6. **Wait, then confirm before clicking Verify.** Run `dig TXT miamipapanoel.com +short`; when your
   string appears, proceed. Usually minutes on Vercel DNS, up to 24 hours elsewhere. Clicking Verify
   too early and failing is harmless — just click again later.

7. **Verify**, then go to **Sitemaps** in the left sidebar, enter just `sitemap.xml`, and Submit.

8. **Belt and braces:** add `Sitemap: https://miamipapanoel.com/sitemap.xml` as a line in
   `public/robots.txt` and redeploy. Thirty seconds.

9. **Don't panic on 1 September.** "Couldn't fetch" for a few hours after submission is normal. Check
   back in 48 hours; if it still fails, the cause is almost always that `sitemap.xml` isn't actually
   being served — back to step 1.

10. **One high-value follow-up:** in Search Console open **URL Inspection**, paste
    `https://miamipapanoel.com/`, click **Request Indexing**. Do the same for your top three service
    pages. It's the fastest lever available on a site that has to rank before December.
