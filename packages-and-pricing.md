# Packages and Pricing

These are suggested ranges. Adjust based on travel distance, date, time, and demand.

## Family Visit

Best for homes, birthdays, small family parties, and surprise visits.

- 20-30 minutes.
- Photos with children and family.
- Short greeting from Santa.
- Optional family-provided gift handoff.
- Public starting price: **$325 first hour**, $150 per extra half hour.

## Event Visit

Best for larger family parties, restaurants, churches, small business events, schools, daycares, and community events.

- 45-60 minutes.
- Photos with guests.
- Santa greeting and interaction.
- Gift handoff if gifts are prepared and labeled.
- Public starting price: **$450 first hour** (corporate). HOA / community **$550 for 2 hours**, two-hour minimum. School or daycare, weekday daytime, **$275 per hour**.

Larger residential communities, schools, business activations, and long events should be quoted higher after details are reviewed.

## Christmas Eve Gift Delivery

Best for high-value bookings on December 24.

- Santa arrives with family-provided gifts.
- Short magical visit and photos.
- Time windows should be tightly scheduled.
- Public starting price: **$500 per 45-minute slot** until 8pm. After 9pm "sneak-a-peek" **$375 for 15 minutes**. Sold in slots, not hours.

## Nonprofit and Toy Drive Visit

Best for community events and organizations serving children.

- Discounted or sponsored rate.
- Sponsor can be mentioned in posts.
- Suggested structure: paid by sponsor, discounted for nonprofit, or donated when possible.

## Add-Ons

- Extra time.
- Travel outside core area.
- Christmas Eve premium.
- Photo backdrop setup.
- Assistant/elf coordination.
- Bilingual announcement or short story time.

## Booking Terms

- Require deposit to hold date and time.
- Final payment due before or at arrival.
- Client provides parking instructions.
- Gifts must be clearly labeled if Santa is handing them out.
- For public events, client manages line, crowd flow, and child safety.

## Full season rate card — adopted 26 August 2026

Researched against South Florida market rates. The Bash reports average cost-to-hire of **$273 in Miami**
and **$350 in Fort Lauderdale**. GigSalad's live Miami Santa listings start at $275, $300, $350 and $375.

| Tier | Rate | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Entry — "Jingle" | **$195 / 45 min** | Mon–Thu daytime, **Dec 1–18 only. Cap at ~12 bookings.** Trade the discount for a review and a photo release. |
| Standard | **$325 first hour**, $150 per extra half hour | Everything else through Dec 18, and daytimes Dec 19–23 |
| HOA / community event | **$550 for 2 hours** | Two-hour minimum |
| School / daycare | **$275 for 1 hour** | Weekday daytime — fills hours that would otherwise earn nothing |
| Corporate / office party | **$450 first hour** | Budget-funded buyers; they do not haggle |
| Photographer mini-session block | **$600 for 4 hours**, $850 full day | The photographer is the client, not the family |
| **Peak** | **$425 first hour** | **Dec 12, 13, 19, 20 after 4pm. No discounts, no exceptions.** |
| **Christmas Eve** | **$500 per 45-minute visit** | Dec 24 until 8pm. Sold in slots, not hours — 5–6 slots is $2,500–$3,000 in one evening. |
| Christmas Eve late | **$375 for 15 minutes** | After 9pm "sneak-a-peek" — gift drop while the child peeks |

**Deposit: 50%, non-refundable, due at booking.** Balance on arrival. One free reschedule if a named
storm or Papa Noel himself forces it — that clause removes the main objection to a non-refundable deposit.

**The rule that does not bend: a date is not booked until the deposit clears. Never discount the peak dates.**

Why $325 and not $200: bilingual is genuinely scarce here. Of roughly sixteen Santas listed across Miami,
Hialeah and Fort Lauderdale, **one** advertises Spanish fluency — in a majority-Hispanic county. That does
not earn a surcharge line item; it wins the booking at full rate.
