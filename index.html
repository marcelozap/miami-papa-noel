A few helpful answers

Do you serve outside Doral?

Yes. We visit Miami-Doral, Hialeah, Kendall, Sweetwater, Miami Lakes, and Coral Gables. Travel may affect pricing.

Can Santa deliver gifts?

Yes. You provide the gifts, clearly labeled with each child's name.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A 50% non-refundable deposit is required, with the balance due on arrival. We confirm the calendar and visit details before your booking is finalized.

Can nonprofits book a visit?

Yes. Nonprofit, toy drive, and sponsor-supported options may be available depending on the date and event.
